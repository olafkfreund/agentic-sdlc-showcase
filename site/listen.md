Two speakers work through why the bottleneck moved, why vendor neutrality became a control
requirement rather than a preference, and what it means to put the decision to allow or
block into deterministic code instead of a review meeting.

It covers roughly what §1 to §4 of the playbook cover. It does not cover the stage plays,
the autonomy matrix or the Substitution Test in any depth — for those, the
[playbook]({{ site.baseurl }}/playbook/) is the thing.

### Two practical notes

**Nothing downloads until you press play.** The player requests metadata only, so landing
here costs a few kilobytes rather than thirteen megabytes.

**Playback stops if you navigate.** This is a static multi-page site, not an application —
following a link to another page reloads it and the audio ends. Open the playbook in a
second tab if you want to read along, or keep this tab playing and browse in another.
